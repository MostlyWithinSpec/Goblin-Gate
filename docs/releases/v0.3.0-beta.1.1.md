A fix for 0.3.0-beta.1: downloads through the IPS no longer stall and fail. Nothing else changed.

This is a beta. Boxes on the stable channel stay on 0.2.0; pick **beta** under System → Updates to try it.

## Fixed

- **Downloads stopped with a connection error when the IPS was on.** When Suricata missed a single packet of
  a connection (a packet lost on the way, or one let through uninspected while Suricata was busy), it
  dropped the whole connection, even with a profile that only alerts. Large and parallel downloads stalled
  and then failed. Now only IPS rules drop traffic: a connection Suricata can't follow completely carries
  on, the same way traffic keeps flowing when Suricata falls behind.

## Upgrading

Boxes on the beta channel get it with the next update check, or at once under System → Updates. The IPS
restarts with the new setting; connections open at that moment carry on.
